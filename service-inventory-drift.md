# Service Inventory Drift

As AI workflow systems grow, their operational surfaces expand faster than their internal maps.

New:
- tools
- adapters
- runtime routes
- integrations
- orchestration layers

appear continuously.

But inventory systems are often treated as static documentation instead of operational infrastructure.

Over time this creates inventory drift:
the system evolves, but its own operational understanding becomes outdated.

This becomes dangerous because:
- governance weakens
- review quality degrades
- hidden operational surfaces appear
- onboarding becomes harder
- continuity recovery slows down

The problem is not usually missing implementation.

The problem is operational visibility decay.

A mature AI workflow eventually needs:
- structural inventory
- semantic mapping
- runtime surface awareness
- governance visibility
- continuity-aware operational tracking

Operational self-awareness is not execution authority.

But systems that lack operational self-awareness eventually lose continuity.

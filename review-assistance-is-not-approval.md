# Review Assistance Is Not Approval

## Governance Problem

An assistant can improve formatting, structure, and clarity without being
authorized to decide whether the work should be released.

## Boundary Principle

Review assistance may improve a draft. Approval must remain a separate human
decision.

## Risk If Violated

Polished output can create false confidence. A clean draft may still contain
reputation, governance, or exposure risk.

## Safe Interpretation

Separate preparation from approval. Better language is not permission to
publish.

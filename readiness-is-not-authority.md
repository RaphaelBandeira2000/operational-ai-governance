# Readiness Is Not Authority

## Governance Problem

AI-assisted systems can make an action look technically ready before the action
has been approved.

## Boundary Principle

Readiness should improve review quality. It should not become execution
permission.

## Risk If Violated

If readiness becomes authority, review turns into ceremony and automation
pressure can bypass human judgment.

## Safe Interpretation

Treat readiness signals as input for human review, not as approval to execute,
publish, commit, deploy, or spend.
